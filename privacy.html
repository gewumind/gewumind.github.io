Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This English version applies to purchases through Creem. For purchases through Mianbaoduo, the Chinese version applies. If the versions conflict, the version applicable to the platform used for the purchase governs.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how GewuMind, an individual operator based in the People’s Republic of China (“GewuMind”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit https://gewumind.com or purchase our software licenses and digital products.

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Name, email address, and contact details you provide;
  • Order ID, purchase platform, product, version, and purchase history;
  • Machine code, license ID, activation status, and support communications;
  • Payment-related information processed by third-party payment providers (we do not store full card details);
  • Browser type, IP address, device information, and basic usage data;
  • Information you provide when contacting support or requesting a refund.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process orders and deliver digital products and license files;
  • Verify purchases and manage licenses, versions, and machine codes;
  • Provide customer support and handle refunds or disputes;
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized license distribution;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements;
  • Improve our products, website, and services.

4. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality. We use GoatCounter for aggregate traffic analytics. GoatCounter does not use advertising cookies and does not build advertising profiles. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Third-party services

We may use third-party services for payments, hosting, DNS, email routing, email delivery, analytics, and license management. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies. Current providers include:

  • Mianbaoduo for CNY payments and digital delivery;
  • Creem for USD payments and Merchant of Record services;
  • GitHub Pages for website hosting;
  • Cloudflare for DNS, security, and email routing;
  • Our email service provider, currently Foxmail/Tencent, for support email;
  • GoatCounter for aggregate website analytics.

6. International transfers

Some service providers are located outside your country. Where required, we use reasonable safeguards for cross-border transfers of personal information. By using the website or purchasing products, you understand that your information may be processed in countries other than your own.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements. Order, license, and tax records may be retained for longer periods where required by law.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your order information, email account, license file, and machine secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, contact support@gewumind.com. We may ask for information to verify your identity and order.

10. Children’s privacy

Our products are not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the website or products after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
