Contact GewuMind

Email support · usually within one business day

For license requests, refunds, or privacy requests, email support@gewumind.com. Please do not send passwords, private keys, or full payment-card details.

Email

support@gewumind.com
Website: https://gewumind.com

We usually reply within one business day. Support is provided in Chinese and English.

License requests

Include:

  • Order ID and purchase platform (Mianbaoduo or Creem);
  • Purchased version line (1.x or 2.x);
  • Machine code shown by the product;
  • The email address used for the purchase, if available.

Licenses are bound to one machine code and one version line. A different machine code or version requires a new license. We do not reissue or transfer licenses.

Refunds

If no license file has been issued, you may request a refund within 7 days of purchase. Once a license file has been issued, sales are final except for duplicate payment, failure to deliver, a material mismatch with the product description, or where required by law. Machine-code changes are not a refund reason.

Include the order ID, purchase platform, and reason in your email.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, email support@gewumind.com. We may ask for information to verify your identity and order.
